About The Performer

Mastering the Fine Art of Voice Illusion

Narendra Shiwale is a celebrated mimicry artist, stand-up performer, and voice illusionist with over a decade of captivating audiences across India and internationally. From iconic vintage Bollywood actors like Amitabh Bachchan, Nana Patekar, and Rajkumar to legendary political figures and modern celebrities, Narendra effortlessly switches between voices within milliseconds.

His shows are not merely voice imitations—they are complete theatrical experiences blend with sharp observational humor, audience interaction, dramatic storytelling, and musical comedy caricatures.
